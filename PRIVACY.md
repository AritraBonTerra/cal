# Cal privacy policy

Last updated November 11, 2026.

Cal is a calendar, task manager and habit tracker for Mac, iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what Cal stores and where it goes.

## On your device

Cal keeps your tasks, time blocks, habits and settings in a database on the device. If you don't sign in, that data stays on the device.

Calendar events come from the calendars already set up on your device, read and edited through Apple's calendar access. Cal never uploads your calendar events. If you choose to write time blocks to one of your calendars, they go to that calendar like any other event.

## When you sign in

Signing in with Google is optional. It turns on sync between your devices. When you sign in:

- Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with Cal, which uses them to identify your account.
- Cal stores your tasks, time blocks, habits and settings on [Convex](https://www.convex.dev), its hosted database, so your other devices can sync them.

Calendar events still stay on each device.

## What Cal doesn't do

Cal has no ads, analytics or tracking, and it doesn't sell or share your data with anyone except the services above, which Cal needs to work.

## Beta testing

If you test Cal through TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports, the feedback you send, and basic device information with the developer, as described in [Apple's TestFlight terms](https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/testflight/).

## Deleting your data

On iPhone and iPad, deleting the app removes its data from that device. On the Mac, quit Cal and delete `~/Library/Application Support/Cal` as well as the app. To delete your synced account data, open an issue at [github.com/AritraBonTerra/cal](https://github.com/AritraBonTerra/cal/issues) and it will be removed.

## Changes
